# Code of Conduct

PsychConnect welcomes respectful participation from people with different backgrounds, experience levels, and viewpoints.

Be considerate, patient, constructive, and focused on the work. Do not harass, insult, threaten, discriminate against, or publish private information about other participants. Keep feedback about ideas and code specific and useful.

Project maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation for conduct that harms the community. Report concerns privately to the repository maintainer through GitHub. For security vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
