# Changelog

## Android 0.6.8 / server 0.2.11

- Solo and co-op Survival score only movement-active time. The timer and difficulty progression pause with the hazards while players are still.

## Android 0.6.7 / server 0.2.10

Current prototype snapshot. This is not a signed store release.

- Versus Deathmatch with alternating roles, haptics, Sonar, Decoys, scoring, rankings, and rivalry records.
- Together challenges: 2 Player Survival and Puzzle.
- Solo Survival with local and online top-five records.
- Harmony multitouch sandbox, optional bilateral shared haptics and trails, rotation-sensitive feedback, and experimental adaptive palm contact.
- Pseudonymous profiles, recovery codes, PostgreSQL persistence, leaderboards, Friends & Foes, achievements, and challenges.
- Animated menu atmosphere, profile avatars, button feedback, and accessibility controls for motion and effects.
- Functional Samsung phone/tablet testing has been reported by the maintainer; broad device certification and blind haptic evaluation remain outstanding.
